# Enterprise Features Missing from Prototype

The target enterprise backlog must include, at minimum:

- tenant provisioning, billing/entitlements, feature flags, support access, and tenant export;
- identity provider integration, MFA, password/session policy, service accounts, and access review;
- dynamic masters, custom fields, import/export, effective dating, and configuration versioning;
- workflow designer/runtime, delegation, escalation, approval matrix, and segregation of duties;
- immutable audit, field history, status history, login events, configuration history, and retention;
- document templates, numbering, rendering, attachments, signatures, secure storage, and reprint history;
- notification templates, in-app/email/SMS providers, recipient rules, escalation, and preferences;
- full inventory ledger, lot genealogy, reservations, storage, quality hold, reversal, and reconciliation;
- production mass balance, route snapshots, loss reasons, OEE, downtime, and integration with inventory;
- quality specs, sampling, laboratory, evaluation, hold/release, deviation, CAPA, and reports;
- maintenance plans, breakdowns, work orders, permits, parts, calibration, and reliability;
- sustainability traceability, certificates, claims, mass balance, due diligence, and evidence;
- governed reporting, KPI definitions, read models, scheduled exports, and audit reporting;
- external integrations, transactional outbox, retry/dead-letter queues, reconciliation, monitoring;
- operations: PostgreSQL migrations, backup/restore, DR, observability, deployment, rollback, and support runbooks.
