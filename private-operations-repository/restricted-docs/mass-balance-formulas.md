# Restricted Engineering Formulas: Palm Oil Mill Mass Balance

## 1. Sterilization Mass Balance
- Standard FFB Steam Absorption: $12\% - 15\%$ on weight.
- Sterilizer Condensate Generation: $W_{condensate} = 0.135 \times W_{FFB}$.
- Oil Loss in Condensate Target: $\le 0.85\%$ on dry basis.

## 2. Decanter Separation Ratios
- Heavy Phase (Aqueous effluent): $50\% - 55\%$ by volume.
- Light Phase (Oil rich): $35\% - 40\%$ routed to clarification tank.
- Solid Phase (Decanter cake): $10\% - 15\%$ routed for boiler fuel / soil conditioner.
