# ROCKEYE Policy Engine

**Classification:** PRIVATE CORE REPOSITORY — PROPRIETARY INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY  
**Owning Domain:** Platform / Policy Governance  
**Scope:** Multi-level approval workflows, statutory compliance rules, and operational threshold policies  

## 1. Overview

The Policy Engine determines authorization routing and operational governance rules:
1. **Dynamic Approval Routing:** Routes transactions based on monetary value, quantity thresholds, and risk score.
2. **Operational Policies:**
   - Weighbridge Weight Tolerance Policy: Flags tare weight discrepancies outside historical vehicle baselines (+/- 2%).
   - Quality Hold Policy: Automatically freezes inventory lot dispatch if lab samples fail critical specifications.
